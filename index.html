Frequently Asked Questions

Questions About Your Bathroom Remodel?

How long will my bathroom remodel take?

Most tub-to-shower conversions and shower replacements are completed in a single day. Full bathroom remodels typically take 2–3 days depending on scope. We give you a clear timeline before we start — no open-ended "it'll take as long as it takes" answers.

What's included in your lifetime warranty?

Our lifetime warranty covers all materials and labor for as long as you own your home. If anything we installed develops an issue, we'll fix it at zero cost to you — no questions asked. It's in writing, not just a verbal promise.

Do you build showers that are safe for seniors?

Absolutely — this is one of our specialties. We install zero-threshold (curbless) entries, built-in bench seats, and grab bars anchored into wall studs (not just drywall). We follow ADA guidelines and can customize the layout for your loved one's specific needs. We've helped dozens of families keep aging parents safely at home.

What areas do you serve?

We serve homeowners across Sacramento, Placer, El Dorado, Yolo, Solano, Napa, Sonoma, Contra Costa, Alameda, San Joaquin, and surrounding counties. If you're unsure, just call us at (916) 892-0745 and we'll confirm right away.

How much does a bathroom remodel cost?

Costs vary depending on scope, materials, and your bathroom's current condition. We offer flat-rate quotes — the number we give you after the in-home visit is the final number. No surprise line items. Financing is available from as low as $99/month. The only way to get an accurate number for your bathroom is a free in-home visit — which we'll schedule at your convenience.

How is MTR Baths different from a big franchise company?

Big franchise companies use commissioned sales reps and often subcontract the actual work. With MTR Baths, you speak directly with Alex (the owner) from your first call to the final walkthrough. Our crew are our employees — not subs — and we've served NorCal for 15+ years. You're not a transaction to us; you're a neighbor.
