Common Questions

Everything You're Wondering About Your Shower Remodel

How long will my shower remodel take?
Most tub-to-shower conversions and shower replacements are completed in just 1–2 days once materials are on-site. Full bathroom remodels typically take 3–5 days. We'll give you an exact timeline at your free in-home consultation.
What's included in your lifetime warranty?
Our lifetime warranty covers all labor and materials for as long as you own your home. If any issues arise with your new shower — cracking, leaking, grout failure — we'll fix it at no charge. No asterisks, no fine print.
Do you do senior and disability-friendly showers?
Yes — this is one of our specialties. We install zero-threshold showers, properly stud-mounted grab bars, fold-down seats, roll-in wheelchair accessible showers, and anti-slip flooring. We've completed over 100 accessible shower projects across Northern California.
What areas do you serve?
We serve all of Northern California including Sacramento, Placer, El Dorado, Yolo, Solano, Napa, Sonoma, Contra Costa, Alameda, San Joaquin, Stanislaus, and surrounding counties. Not sure if we serve your area? Call us at (916) 892-0745.
How much does a shower remodel cost?
Tub-to-shower conversions typically range from $2,800–$6,500. Walk-in showers run $3,500–$9,000. Full bathroom remodels start around $6,500. Every quote we provide is flat-rate and all-inclusive — no surprise fees. With our Spring Special you save $2,500 off any project.
Do you offer financing?
Yes! We offer flexible financing with quick approval and monthly payments as low as $99/month. Ask about your options when you book your free in-home consultation.
