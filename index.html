Frequently Asked Questions

How long does a bathroom remodel take?+
Most tub-to-shower conversions are done in a single day. Full bathroom remodels typically take 2–3 days. We give you a clear timeline before we start — no surprises.
Is the estimate really free?+
Yes, 100% free. We come to your home, measure everything, and give you a flat-rate quote. No obligation, no pressure, no charge.
What does the lifetime warranty cover?+
All labor and materials for as long as you own the home. If anything we installed has an issue, we fix it at zero cost. It's in writing — not just a verbal promise.
Do you do senior-safe / ADA showers?+
Absolutely — it's one of our specialties. We install zero-threshold entries, built-in bench seats, and grab bars bolted into wall studs (not just drywall anchors). Safe and sharp-looking.
Do you offer financing?+
Yes — flexible financing from as low as $99/month. Quick approval, no hidden fees. Ask us about your options during the free in-home estimate.
